How transfers work.

Send 100 SWARM between ordinary wallets: 99 arrives, 1 goes to 0x0000…dEaD. Fractions round down to the smallest token unit. Burned tokens remain in the ERC-20 total supply; “total burned” tracks the automatic transfer charges.

The token has one constructor mint, with no owner, pause, blacklist, or later mint. Launch allocations, contributor claims, and pool settlement arrive in full. The pool price is a live market snapshot; your quote includes liquidity and trading fees.

Known limit of the burn. Sells must reach the PoolManager whole or they cannot settle, and buys leave it whole, so transfers to or from the PoolManager are never charged. Anyone can use that path to move SWARM between wallets without the 1%, or hold it as a PoolManager claim that changes hands off the SWARM ledger. “Total burned” counts only what direct transfers actually burned. The burn cannot be moved onto pool trades without a hook, which this token deliberately does not have.
